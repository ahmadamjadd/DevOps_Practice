#As root mode

sudo find /fcse -name "test_file.txt"

Explanation: Executed Successfully! Root can do anything. It ignores all 770 or ACL restrictions. The search was fast and successful without errors.




#As group member

ssh user1
find /fcse -name "test_file.txt"

Explanation: Since user1 is in the fcse group and the folder is 770, they have r and x access. The search worked perfectly.


#As non-group member

ssh user2
find /fcse -name "test_file.txt"

Explanation: user2 is in the fee group. They have no permissions on /fcse (the "Others" category is 0).
